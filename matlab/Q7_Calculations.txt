<---- Observation for practical record --->
Slotted ALOHA achieves twice the maximum theoretical throughput of Pure ALOHA. 
This is because Slotted ALOHA allows transmissions only at fixed time-slot boundaries, reducing the vulnerable period and the probability of collisions.
